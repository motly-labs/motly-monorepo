Four small but complete interfaces, each the kind of screen people ship. Each one combines several
things the [small examples](../) show one at a time. Open one on its own page for more room.

## Add to cart

A product image flies to the cart along an arc, a ring and a pop of confetti in the product's color
land on the cart, and the count bumps. It is one GSAP timeline across three elements:

- `tl.shape()` and `tl.burst()` sit in the timeline after the flight, placed with GSAP's position
  parameter.
- The cart is an Anchor, and its position is read when its burst starts, not when the timeline is
  built, so resizing the page mid-flight still lands the burst on the cart.
- The pop is a Spec built by a function, `pop(tone)`, from the product's color.
- Under reduced motion the flight is skipped and the bursts show their Resting frame.

## Daily goals

A checklist with a progress ring. Each task bursts from its checkbox when done, and unchecking it
plays that burst backward into the checkbox. The ring bursts at a third, two thirds and all done,
bigger each time.

- Each milestone's Spec is built from the progress in code: more Children, further out, and stars
  at the end. A Spec is plain data.
- `tween.reverse()` on a finished burst runs it back, as it would any GSAP tween.
- The switch at the bottom passes `reducedMotion: 'always'`, to show what a viewer who prefers
  reduced motion sees: each burst's Resting frame, for as long as the burst would have played.

## Year in review

A scroll story in the style of a yearly recap. As each stat scrolls in, its number counts up and
fireworks open behind it; scroll back and both run backward, exactly.

- ScrollTrigger scrubs each section's timeline: the scroll position is the Playhead.
- `container: section` paints each burst inside its section, so it scrolls with it and is clipped
  by it.
- A fixed `seed` per section means the fireworks are the same on every visit. Within one tween they
  never change anyway, however often they are scrubbed.

## Chat reactions, in React

A chat thread where each reaction sends a burst of its own: hearts, confetti or a thumbs-up pop.
Delete a message while its burst is still flying, and the burst goes with it.

- `useGSAP()` gives each message a GSAP context, and `contextSafe()` puts every burst the message
  creates in it. When the message unmounts, the context is reverted and its bursts are cleared.
- The Specs live outside the components: they are data, not state.

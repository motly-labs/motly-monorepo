Each example below is complete: copy its JavaScript and its HTML with the buttons on the code, and
it runs with `@motly/gsap` and `gsap` installed. With script tags instead, drop the imports and use
`Motly.rand` and `Motly.each`; the script build registers itself.

## The pens

Five examples that each show something only a GSAP plugin can do. They are also on
[CodePen](https://codepen.io/collection/kkrLNz), loading everything from a CDN.

### Heart like burst

Timeline sequencing: the heart squashes, then a ring, a pop and sparks, each placed with GSAP's
position parameter. Unliking mid-burst reverts the timeline, which clears it.
[Open on CodePen](https://codepen.io/realdreamer/pen/01a0f289-4ec7-7fcd-ae21-06a37c3c4e83).

<Example name="pen-heart" height={320} />

### Confetti cannon

Three bursts in one timeline: from the button, then from both sides of the screen at once.
[Open on CodePen](https://codepen.io/realdreamer/pen/01a0f2aa-ce1a-7cdf-820c-86e1bac0b437).

<Example name="pen-confetti" height={360} />

### Scroll-scrubbed fireworks

ScrollTrigger scrubs a timeline of rockets and shells, back and forth. The shells are painted in
the pinned section, so they scroll with it. Scroll inside the example.
[Open on CodePen](https://codepen.io/realdreamer/pen/01a0f2af-63a4-70e2-aeeb-53aa7f193ecd).

<Example name="pen-firework" height={420} />

### Click sparkles

Every click bursts from its own point.
[Open on CodePen](https://codepen.io/realdreamer/pen/01a0f2b2-4955-7f79-8b31-5c6483dcb099).

<Example name="pen-sparkle" height={300} />

### Reversible ripple toggle

Clicking again mid-ripple reverses the timeline, and the ripple runs back into the button.
[Open on CodePen](https://codepen.io/realdreamer/pen/01a0f2b7-a4b9-73ea-a610-47e1627e7d1e).

<Example name="pen-ripple" height={320} />

## Small ideas

### Sparkle on hover

A burst around a card as the pointer enters it. Reverting the last one first keeps quick hovers
from piling up.

<Example name="ex-hover" height={280} />

### Copied

A ring and a few dots confirm a click, and the label changes back once the timeline reaches 1.5
seconds.

<Example name="ex-copy" height={240} />

### Unread ping

A ring that repeats until the notification is read. `revert()` stops it and clears it mid-flight.

<Example name="ex-ping" height={280} />

### Jelly pop

A [path Curve](../reference/values/#curves) copied from a design tool: it overshoots to 120%, falls
back to 90% and settles.
